# Round 1, Activity 3: Impact (form rubric, copied 20 Nov)

**Limit:** 50-500 words. **Due:** 27 Nov. Up to 25 points. The team lead (Ritankar) submits.

Form fields: Who benefits, and roughly how many / What changes compared with today / How you'd know it worked / The main risk.

What the answer should cover:
- **Who benefits.** The people whose day changes, and roughly how many.
- **What changes.** What changes for them compared with how they manage today.
- **How you'd know it worked.** A measurable signal, ideally with a target.
- **The main risk.** The biggest risk or limit, stated honestly.

Evaluation: an AI check reads the answer ("a real description of your idea's impact"); if it passes, Round 1 is complete.
Scoring weighs **who benefits and what changes most**, then **how you'd measure it**, then **the main risk**.
