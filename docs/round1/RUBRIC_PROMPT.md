# Round 1, Activity 2 (Prompt): the form's own guidance

*Copied from the dashboard by Ritankar, 15 Nov. 80-800 words, due 27 Nov (Round 1 closes 26 Nov: go by that).*

Write the prompt you would give an AI app builder to create a working prototype of your idea: the users, the voice
flow step by step, what it says back, and how it handles noise, accents and mixed languages.

Form headings: Your users and their languages / The voice flow, step by step / What the product says or shows back /
Noise, accents, mixed languages and misheard input.

**What your prompt should cover**
- Your users. Who they are, and the languages and accents they speak in.
- The voice flow, step by step. What people say at each step, and what the product says or shows back.
- When things go wrong. Noise, accents, mixed languages and misheard input, and what the product does in each case.
- Enough detail to build. Specific enough that two builders would produce nearly the same prototype.

**How it's evaluated**
- An AI check reads it on submit; passing completes Activity 2 and unlocks Activity 3 (20 Nov).
- It checks that this is a real prompt someone could give an AI app builder.
- Scoring weighs the voice flow most, then how things going wrong are handled, then how buildable it is.
- Up to 50 points toward the Round 1 total.
