# Round 1, Activity 1 (Idea): the form's own guidance

*Copied from the dashboard by Ritankar, 15 Nov.*

**What your idea should cover**
- **The problem.** One specific problem people in India face, for example paying an electricity bill or booking a clinic visit.
- **Who has it, where and how often.** The people who face it, the setting, and how often it hurts.
- **The voice-first product.** What people say to it and what it does, step by step.
- **Why voice comes first.** Why speaking is the main way people would use it, not a feature added on top.

**How the quality check works**
- An AI check reads your idea when you submit. If it passes, Activity 1 is complete and Activity 2 unlocks on 13 Nov.
- It checks that this is a real, specific idea, not empty, a joke, gibberish or a copy of the question.
- It looks most at whether voice is essential to using your product, then at how specific the problem is.
- The same check scores your idea, up to 25 points toward your Round 1 total.
- Between 50 and 500 words.
