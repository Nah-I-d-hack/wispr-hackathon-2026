## Rules that bind us

- **Build by voice with Wispr Flow.** The whole point of the event is "build by talking, not typing". Humans dictate
  through Flow (any Indian language, it switches mid-sentence) into any editor or AI tool (Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT…).
  Check each round's rules for whether we must show proof (screen recording, Flow stats).
- Team: solo or up to **3**. **Team is locked after the first submission.** Decide the roster before the
  Round 1 Idea submission.
- Eligibility: in India, 18+. Free.
- Register with the phone number used to verify the account, and with the **same email you sign in to Wispr Flow with**.
- Round 2 includes an online master class with Wispr.

## Tracks (we chose Healthcare)

Civic services · **Healthcare** · Education · Small business · Everyday access.

Healthcare, as listed: *"Symptom intake in regional languages"*, unlocking *"care for patients who think in their

participants, so our Round 1 healthcare work must stay adaptable.
